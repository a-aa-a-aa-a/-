A young Kid, feeling grown-up, lingered in the pasture while the flock went home. As dusk fell, he realized he was alone and became afraid. Suddenly, he saw a Wolf. In desperation, the Kid asked the Wolf to play a tune so he could dance one last time. The Wolf agreed and began to pipe. The music carried far, and the Shepherd Dogs heard it. Recognizing the Wolf's feast song, they raced back. The Wolf fled, cursing himself for being tricked into playing music when he should have eaten the Kid right away.
