For years, the librarian noticed it. Every Monday, a specific book on local history would migrate from its shelf to a reading table by the west window. It was never checked out. The security footage showed nothing but a brief blur of light at 3 AM, like dust motes catching the moonlight. One evening, she left a note in the book: "Did you find what you were looking for?" The next morning, the book was on its shelf, perfectly placed. Inside, a new sentence had been added in faded ink to a century-old census record: "I found myself. Thank you for looking."
