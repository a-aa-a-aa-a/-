His view was a brick wall. Hers was a forested mountain. Every morning, he logged onto a website where people shared live feeds from their windows. He would sip his coffee, watching mist rise between pine trees on her screen. She, seven time zones away, would brew her evening tea, smiling at the morning sun painting the red bricks gold on his. They never communicated, yet shared a profound, silent exchange of light and landscape. A perfect, private friendship built on the simple gift of a view.
